## Overview

Supported Professor Mattia Fazzini in delivering an undergraduate course by facilitating lab discussions, refining instructional materials, and assessing student projects for a class of 150 students.


## Responsibilities

- Collaborated closely with Professor Fazzini to analyze and improve lab and assignment instructions, enhancing clarity and the overall learning experience.  
- Led lab discussion sessions, guiding students through assignments and projects while addressing questions and technical challenges.  
- Conducted detailed evaluations of student projects and assignments, providing timely and constructive feedback to support student growth.  
- Assisted in maintaining course standards and ensuring students met learning objectives.


## Reflections

This role strengthened my ability to communicate technical concepts effectively and support a large student cohort in a collaborative learning environment. Working directly with faculty enhanced my skills in curriculum refinement and instructional design.


## Skills Developed

- Communication and instruction in a lab setting  
- Curriculum analysis and improvement  
- Student assessment and feedback delivery  
- Teamwork and collaboration with faculty


## Additional Notes

- Worked under the mentorship of Professor Mattia Fazzini  
- Contributed to continuous course improvement initiatives
